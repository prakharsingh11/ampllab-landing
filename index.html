1 — Mission

We are an audio data research lab based in India, focused on conversational speech. We capture natural dialogue with the precision needed to train the next generation of voice AI.

2 — Process

We engineer audio datasets with the same rigor labs bring to models.

i.

Identify

Determine a voice AI capability that requires new speech data to unlock.

ii.

Design

Architect the shape of audio data—speakers, channels, metadata—to teach that capability.

iii.

Collect

Launch targeted recording sessions with channel-separated capture across India.

iv.

Process

Run audio through our quality pipeline—enhancement, alignment, validation.

v.

Measure

Evaluate signal quality until a high-fidelity, research-grade dataset emerges.

vi.

Scale

Expand the collection to thousands of hours while maintaining quality standards.

Our Clients

Our speech datasets are used by research labs and product teams building speech recognition, synthesis, translation, and conversational AI systems.

3 — Datasets

Audio dataset collections for voice AI systems

Dialogue

Channel-separated two-speaker conversations across diverse topics. Natural turn-taking with complete speaker isolation for ASR and conversational AI training.

Regional

Conversational speech across Indian English dialects. Metadata includes accent markers, regional tags, and demographic information for diverse TTS training.

Acoustic

Recordings tagged by environment—office, home, outdoor, vehicle. Quality metrics included for noise robustness and speech enhancement research.

Custom

We partner with research teams to design and collect audio data for specific domains, languages, or acoustic requirements. Contact us to discuss.
